# Dragon Type Weakness - How to Beat Dragon Pokemon in 2026

Dragon types are some of the most powerful Pokemon in the game. Garchomp, Dragonite, Rayquaza, Dragapult — they dominate competitive play with high stats and excellent coverage. But every Dragon has a weakness.

## Dragon Type Weaknesses

Dragon is weak to exactly 3 types:

1. **Ice (2×)** — The most reliable Dragon counter
2. **Dragon (2×)** — Dragons beat Dragons, but it's a double-edged sword
3. **Fairy (2×)** — Complete immunity to Dragon moves + super effective back

## Why Fairy is the Best Dragon Counter

Fairy type is **immune** to Dragon moves. This is huge. A Garchomp locked into Outrage does literally 0 damage to any Fairy type.

Best Fairy counters for Dragons:
- **Sylveon** — Pixilate Hyper Voice destroys Dragons. 130 SpDef tanks Dragon Pulse.
- **Clefable** — Magic Guard + Unaware ignores Dragon Dance boosts. Moonblast OHKOs most Dragons.
- **Gardevoir** — Fairy/Psychic with Moonblast and Psychic coverage.
- **Togekiss** — Air Slash flinch + Dazzling Gleam. Serene Grace makes it annoying.
- **Magearna** — Steel/Fairy resists Dragon AND is immune to it. Fleur Cannon hits like a truck.

## Why Ice Moves are Essential

You don't need an Ice-type Pokemon — you just need Ice-type moves. Many Water types learn Ice Beam, making them excellent Dragon checks:

- **Ice Beam on Blastoise/Swampert** — Surprise KO on Garchomp and Dragonite
- **Ice Shard on Mamoswine** — Priority move picks off weakened Dragons
- **Freeze-Dry on Lapras** — Hits Water/Dragon types (Palkia, Kingdra) super effectively
- **Ice Punch on Weavile** — Fastest Ice attacker, revenge kills most Dragons

### The 4× Ice Weakness Club

Some Dragon types take **4× damage** from Ice:
- **Garchomp** (Dragon/Ground) — Ice Beam = instant KO
- **Dragonite** (Dragon/Flying) — Ice Shard can OHKO
- **Rayquaza** (Dragon/Flying) — The most extreme case
- **Salamence** (Dragon/Flying) — Same as Dragonite
- **Landorus** (Ground/Flying) — Not Dragon, but same 4× Ice weakness

If you see any of these on your opponent's team, bring Ice coverage. Period.

## Dragon vs Dragon

Using Dragon moves against Dragons is effective but risky — you take super effective damage too. Best Dragon-slaying Dragons:

- **Dragapult** (Dragon/Ghost) — 142 Speed outspeeds almost every Dragon. Dragon Darts + Shadow Ball.
- **Garchomp** — Outrage hits hard, but getting locked in is dangerous against Fairy switches.
- **Kyurem-Black** — 170 Attack with Fusion Bolt + Dragon Claw covers everything.

## Countering Specific Dragons

### How to Beat Garchomp
- **Best counter:** Any Fairy type (immune to Outrage/Dragon Claw)
- **Ice Beam** from any Water type OHKOs (4× weak)
- **Watch out for:** Swords Dance + Scale Shot, Stone Edge for Ice types
- [Full Garchomp guide →](/pokemon/garchomp)

### How to Beat Dragonite
- **Best counter:** Ice Shard from Mamoswine or Weavile (4× weak, bypasses Multiscale with prior damage)
- **Stealth Rock** strips Multiscale on switch-in
- **Fairy types** wall it completely
- **Watch out for:** Dragon Dance + Extreme Speed priority
- [Full Dragonite guide →](/pokemon/dragonite)

### How to Beat Dragapult
- **Best counter:** Dark types (Tyranitar, Greninja) — resist Ghost STAB
- **Fairy types** — immune to Dragon, resist nothing else from Dragapult though
- **Watch out for:** Extremely fast (142 Speed), mixed attacker, U-turn pivoting
- [Full Dragapult guide →](/pokemon/dragapult)

### How to Beat Salamence
- **Best counter:** Ice moves (4× weak), Fairy types
- **Stealth Rock** chips 25% on every switch
- **Watch out for:** Dragon Dance + Moxie snowballing, Mega Salamence's Aerilate
- [Full Salamence guide →](/pokemon/salamence)

## Best Anti-Dragon Team Core

Build your team with at least 2 of these to handle Dragons:

1. **Fairy type** (Sylveon, Clefable, or Magearna) — Your primary Dragon check
2. **Ice coverage** (Ice Beam on a Water type) — Backup for 4× weak Dragons
3. **Steel type** (Ferrothorn, Corviknight) — Resists Dragon, provides hazards

Example core:
- **Clefable** (Fairy) — Unaware walls Dragon Dance sweepers
- **Swampert** (Water/Ground) — Ice Beam for Garchomp, resists Rock moves
- **Ferrothorn** (Grass/Steel) — Stealth Rock + Leech Seed, resists Dragon

## Dragon Type in Pokemon GO

In Pokemon GO raids, Dragon types are best countered by:

1. **Mega Gardevoir** — Charm + Dazzling Gleam (Fairy)
2. **Shadow Mamoswine** — Powder Snow + Avalanche (Ice)
3. **Mega Rayquaza** — Dragon Tail + Outrage (Dragon vs Dragon)
4. **Shadow Dragonite** — Dragon Tail + Outrage
5. **Galarian Darmanitan** — Ice Fang + Avalanche

For GO Battle League PvP:
- **Togekiss** dominates Dragon types in Master League
- **Azumarill** (Water/Fairy) walls Dragons in Great League
- **Alolan Ninetales** (Ice/Fairy) double-counters Dragons

## Key Takeaways

1. **Always carry Ice coverage** — Ice Beam on a Water type costs you nothing and handles most Dragons
2. **Fairy types are mandatory** — At least one Fairy on every competitive team
3. **Stealth Rock matters** — 25% chip on Dragonite/Salamence/Charizard every switch
4. **Don't rely on Dragon vs Dragon** — It's a coin flip; Fairy is safer
5. **Scout for coverage moves** — Good Dragon players carry Poison Jab (for Fairies) or Iron Head (for Fairies)
