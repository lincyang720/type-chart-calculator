## 60-second script

Think a Pokémon matchup works both ways? It doesn't. Take Ground and Electric. A Ground move hits an Electric-type Pokémon for double damage. But Electric moves do zero damage against pure Ground. The arrow matters: move type first, defending type second.

We checked all pairs among the eighteen modern types. There are one hundred fifty-three different-type pairs. Seventy-four behave differently when you reverse the direction.

Look at Fire and Steel: Fire moves hit Steel for double damage, but Steel moves are resisted by Fire. Want to check two defending types? Look up one attacking move against each defending type, and multiply the two factors.
