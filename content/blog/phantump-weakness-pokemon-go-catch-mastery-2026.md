# Phantump Weakness in Pokémon GO: Ghost/Grass Catch Mastery Guide

**Phantump Catch Mastery** is scheduled for **September 26, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. local time**. Pokémon GO says Phantump will appear through Field Research plus free and paid Timed Research. Phantump is **Ghost/Grass**, so it does not behave like a pure Ghost or Grass defender. [Read the official announcement](https://pokemongo.com/news/catch-mastery-phantump-2026).

This guide separates confirmed event details from the standard type-chart answer. It does not assume an unannounced raid, moveset, or battle role.

**Officially confirmed:** Pokémon GO lists Phantump in Field Research, free Timed Research, and paid Timed Research during Catch Mastery. It also says all Timed Research tasks and rewards must be completed and claimed by **8:00 p.m. local time** on September 26.

## Five weaknesses, not one automatic answer

The chart gives Phantump five 2× weaknesses, but that does not make Fire, Flying, Ice, Ghost, and Dark interchangeable. Dark is worth checking early when survivability matters because it pressures Ghost while having a favorable standard-chart relationship with Ghost damage. Ghost can provide the same super-effective result but may be exposed to return Ghost pressure. Fire, Flying, and Ice are equally valid chart answers through the Grass half; their value turns on the particular Pokémon’s moves, level, and defensive profile.

That is why this guide does not name one permanent “best” counter. In Pokémon GO, live moves, stats, weather, shields, raid rules, and your roster decide whether a chart-correct option is actually practical.
