# Pokemon GO Type Chart 2026 - Quick Reference Guide

Pokemon GO uses a slightly different type chart than the main series games. Understanding these differences is crucial for raids, PvP (GO Battle League), and Team GO Rocket battles.

## How Pokemon GO Type Effectiveness Differs

The main series uses 2×, 0.5×, and 0× multipliers. Pokemon GO is different:

| Matchup | Main Series | Pokemon GO |
|---------|------------|------------|
| Super Effective | 2× | 1.6× |
| Not Very Effective | 0.5× | 0.625× |
| Immune | 0× | 0.391× |
| Double Super Effective | 4× | 2.56× |
| Double Resist | 0.25× | 0.391× |

**Key difference:** There are no true immunities in Pokemon GO. Ground moves still hit Flying types, Normal moves still hit Ghost types — they just deal reduced damage (0.391×).

This means some strategies from the main series don't work in GO. For example, Gengar (Ghost/Poison) still takes damage from Normal and Fighting moves.

## GO Battle League (PvP) Type Tips

PvP in Pokemon GO is all about type coverage and shield management. Here are the key meta insights:

### Great League (1500 CP)
- **Medicham** (Fighting/Psychic) — Counter + Ice Punch/Psychic dominates
- **Azumarill** (Water/Fairy) — Bubble + Ice Beam/Play Rough covers everything
- **Registeel** (Steel) — Lock On + Focus Blast/Flash Cannon walls most things
- **Swampert** (Water/Ground) — Mud Shot + Hydro Cannon is incredibly fast

### Ultra League (2500 CP)
- **Giratina Altered** (Ghost/Dragon) — Shadow Claw + Dragon Claw/Shadow Sneak
- **Cresselia** (Psychic) — Psycho Cut + Grass Knot/Moonblast
- **Swampert** (Water/Ground) — Still dominant here
- **Trevenant** (Ghost/Grass) — Shadow Claw + Seed Bomb/Shadow Ball

### Master League (No CP Limit)
- **Dialga** (Steel/Dragon) — Dragon Breath + Iron Head/Draco Meteor
- **Giratina Origin** (Ghost/Dragon) — Shadow Claw + Shadow Ball/Ominous Wind
- **Mewtwo** (Psychic) — Psycho Cut + Psystrike/Shadow Ball
- **Groudon** (Ground) — Mud Shot + Fire Punch/Precipice Blades

## Team GO Rocket Counters

### vs Giovanni
Giovanni's Pokemon rotate, but these types consistently work:
- **Fighting** — Handles most Normal-type shields
- **Ground** — Covers Electric and Poison grunts
- **Fairy** — Walls Dragon-type shadow legendaries

### vs Cliff, Sierra, Arlo
General strategy:
1. Lead with a **fast-charging Pokemon** (Swampert, Lucario) to burn shields
2. Switch to your **type counter** for their main threat
3. Keep a **generalist** (Mewtwo, Garchomp) in the back

## Quick Type Reference

**Best offensive types** (hit the most types super effectively):
1. Fighting — hits 5 types SE
2. Ground — hits 5 types SE
3. Ice — hits 4 types SE (all meta-relevant: Dragon, Flying, Ground, Grass)

**Best defensive types** (fewest weaknesses):
1. Electric — 1 weakness (Ground)
2. Normal — 1 weakness (Fighting) + 1 immunity
3. Steel — 3 weaknesses but 10 resistances

**Most dangerous type combos to face:**
1. Water/Ground — only weak to Grass (Swampert)
2. Steel/Fairy — only weak to Fire and Ground (Magearna)
3. Ghost/Dark — only weak to Fairy (Sableye, Spiritomb)
