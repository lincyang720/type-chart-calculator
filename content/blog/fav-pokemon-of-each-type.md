## How to choose your own favorite for each type

The best personal list does not need to match the table. Use the type as a starting filter, then ask three questions:

1. **Does the design communicate the type?** A strong visual idea makes a favorite easy to remember.
2. **Does the Pokémon play differently from the obvious choice?** A dual type, unusual ability, or different role can make a familiar type more interesting.
3. **Would you actually put it on a team?** A favorite can be chosen for appearance, story, or battle value; the important part is knowing which reason matters to you.

For dual types, decide which type you are using as the lens. For example, Garchomp works for Dragon because of its species concept, but it also gives Ground a more aggressive identity. That is not a conflict: a Pokémon can be a favorite representative of more than one type when the reason is different in each row.
