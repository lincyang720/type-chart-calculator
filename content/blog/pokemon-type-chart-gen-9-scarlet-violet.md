# Pokemon Type Chart Gen 9 Scarlet Violet - Complete Guide 2026

Pokemon Scarlet and Violet introduced **Generation 9** with revolutionary Tera Type mechanics that fundamentally changed competitive battling. This guide covers everything you need to master type matchups in Gen 9.

## What's New in Gen 9?

### Tera Type System

The biggest change in Gen 9 is **Terastallization** - a mechanic that lets Pokemon change their type mid-battle:

- **Every Pokemon has a hidden Tera Type** (can be any of the 18 types)
- **Terastallizing changes your Pokemon's type** to its Tera Type
- **STAB boost increases** from 1.5× to 2× for moves matching Tera Type
- **One-time use per battle** (can't switch out and Tera again)
- **Lasts until the Pokemon faints**

**Example:**
- Charizard (Fire/Flying) with Water Tera Type
- Terastallizes → becomes pure Water type
- Loses Fire/Flying weaknesses, gains Water weaknesses
- Water moves get 2× STAB instead of 1.5×

### New Pokemon & Type Combinations

Gen 9 introduced several new type combinations:

- **Grass/Fire**: Scovillain (first ever!)
- **Normal/Poison**: Grafaiai
- **Poison/Normal**: Maushold line
- **Electric/Fighting**: Pawmot

## Tera Type Strategy Guide

### Offensive Tera Types

**Goal:** Boost your strongest move with 2× STAB

**Example: Dragonite with Normal Tera**
- Extreme Speed becomes 2× STAB (instead of 1.5×)
- Turns Dragonite into a priority nuke
- Common in VGC and OU

**Example: Azumarill with Water Tera**
- Aqua Jet and Liquidation get 2× STAB
- Loses Fairy typing (no longer weak to Poison/Steel)
- Becomes a pure Water-type wallbreaker

### Defensive Tera Types

**Goal:** Remove your biggest weakness

**Example: Garchomp with Fire Tera**
- Loses 4× Ice weakness (biggest threat)
- Becomes Fire type (resists Ice!)
- Can survive Ice Beam and retaliate

**Example: Kingambit with Fairy Tera**
- Loses Fighting weakness (biggest counter)
- Gains Fighting resistance
- Becomes nearly unwallable

### Tera Blast Strategy

**Tera Blast** is a new move in Gen 9:
- Base Power: 80
- Changes type to match your Tera Type
- Uses higher offensive stat (Attack or Special Attack)

**Common Sets:**
- **Espathra** (Psychic) with Fairy Tera → Tera Blast becomes Fairy
- **Dragonite** (Dragon/Flying) with Ground Tera → Tera Blast becomes Ground (hits Electric-types)
- **Gholdengo** (Steel/Ghost) with Fighting Tera → Tera Blast becomes Fighting (hits Dark-types)

## Tera Type Prediction & Counterplay

### How to Predict Tera Types

1. **Check common sets** - Most Pokemon have standard Tera Types
2. **Watch for defensive Teras** - If opponent stays in on a bad matchup, they might Tera
3. **Bait it out** - Force opponent to Tera early, then counter it
4. **Scout with Protect** - Use Protect to see if opponent Terastallizes

### How to Counter Tera Types

**Strategy 1: Force Early Tera**
- Threaten with super effective move
- Opponent Teras to survive
- Switch to a counter for their new type

**Strategy 2: Save Your Tera**
- Don't Tera first
- React to opponent's Tera with your own
- Tera advantage wins games

**Strategy 3: Multi-Type Coverage**
- Run moves that hit multiple types
- Example: Earthquake + Ice Beam + Thunderbolt covers most Teras
- Reduces effectiveness of defensive Teras

## VGC vs Singles: Tera Type Differences

### VGC (Doubles)

- **Offensive Teras dominate** (boost STAB for quick KOs)
- **Tera Blast is rare** (better to use natural STAB)
- **Defensive Teras on support Pokemon** (Amoonguss, Torkoal)
- **Tera timing is critical** (can swing entire game)

### OU Singles

- **Defensive Teras more common** (remove key weaknesses)
- **Tera Blast on special attackers** (Espathra, Gholdengo)
- **Tera prediction is key** (baiting and scouting)
- **Tera advantage = win condition** (save it for late game)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Tera-ing Too Early

**Mistake:** Tera on Turn 1 without thinking
**Fix:** Save Tera for critical moments (late game sweeps, surviving key hits)

### 2. Predictable Tera Types

**Mistake:** Always using the same Tera Type
**Fix:** Mix it up. Use unexpected Teras to surprise opponents.

### 3. Ignoring Opponent's Tera

**Mistake:** Not tracking when opponent can Tera
**Fix:** Always assume opponent has Tera available. Play around it.

### 4. Bad Tera Type Choices

**Mistake:** Using Ice Tera on a defensive Pokemon
**Fix:** Match Tera Type to your Pokemon's role (offensive vs defensive)
