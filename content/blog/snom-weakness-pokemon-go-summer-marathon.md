# Snom Weakness in Pokémon GO: Summer Marathon 2026 Guide

## Finding Snom during Arctic Embers

The official event announcement provides two specified paths for Snom. First, it says Snom may appear when you complete **GO Pass tasks**. Second, it lists Snom among the Pokémon that can hatch from **5 km Eggs**. Neither line states an encounter rate, Egg probability, task requirement, or number of encounters needed to find a Shiny Snom, so those should not be treated as guaranteed.

The event’s GO Pass is available from August 4 at 10:00 a.m. to August 10 at 8:00 p.m. local time. Pokémon GO says all Trainers will automatically receive the free GO Pass: Summer Marathon at the beginning of that window. Rewards unlocked in the pass expire on August 12 at 8:00 p.m. local time, which is a separate deadline from the event itself.

If you are pursuing Eggs, the announcement also says its Tier 2 GO Pass bonus starts at Rank 20 and reduces Egg Hatch Distance for Eggs placed in an Incubator during the event. That is an announced bonus, but it does not confirm Snom odds or change the standard type matchup above.
