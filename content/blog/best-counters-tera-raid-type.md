# Best Counters for Every Tera Raid Type - Pokemon Scarlet Violet 2026

Tera Raids are the ultimate endgame challenge in Pokemon Scarlet and Violet. This guide covers the **best counters for every Tera Raid type**, including optimal Pokemon, moves, builds, and strategies for 5-star and 6-star raids.

## How Tera Raids Work

### Raid Mechanics

- **Tera Shield**: Raid bosses have a shield that must be broken
- **Time Limit**: 5 minutes to defeat the boss
- **Stat Boosts**: Raid bosses get stat boosts throughout the battle
- **Tera Type**: Boss Terastallizes at the start (determines weaknesses)
- **Multiplayer**: Up to 4 players (or 3 NPCs if solo)

### Key Strategies

1. **Bring super effective moves** - 2× damage breaks shields faster
2. **Use support moves** - Screech, Acid Spray, Helping Hand
3. **Stack stat boosts** - Swords Dance, Nasty Plot, Iron Defense
4. **Coordinate with team** - Don't all attack at once (waste turns)
5. **Save Tera for late game** - Use it when boss is low HP

## Advanced Raid Strategies

### Solo vs Multiplayer

**Solo Raids:**
- NPCs are unreliable (use weak moves)
- Bring a self-sufficient Pokemon (Azumarill, Iron Hands)
- Use support moves yourself (Screech, Helping Hand)
- Takes longer but more consistent

**Multiplayer Raids:**
- Coordinate with team (don't all attack at once)
- One player uses support moves (Screech, Acid Spray)
- Others stack damage
- Much faster but requires communication

### Support Moves Tier List

**S-Tier (Always Useful):**
- **Screech** - Lowers Defense by 2 stages (helps physical attackers)
- **Acid Spray** - Lowers Special Defense by 2 stages (helps special attackers)
- **Helping Hand** - Boosts ally's damage by 1.5× (multiplayer only)

**A-Tier (Situational):**
- **Light Screen** - Halves Special damage for 5 turns
- **Reflect** - Halves Physical damage for 5 turns
- **Tailwind** - Doubles Speed for 4 turns

**B-Tier (Niche):**
- **Trick Room** - Reverses Speed order (for slow attackers)
- **Rain Dance / Sunny Day** - Boosts Water/Fire moves by 1.5×
- **Electric Terrain / Grassy Terrain** - Boosts Electric/Grass moves by 1.5×

### Item Recommendations

**Best Raid Items:**
1. **Shell Bell** - Heals 1/8 of damage dealt (sustain)
2. **Leftovers** - Heals 1/16 HP per turn (passive healing)
3. **Life Orb** - 1.3× damage boost (aggressive)
4. **Metronome** - Stacks damage for repeated moves
5. **Punching Glove** - 1.1× punch move power (Iron Hands)

### EV Spread Recommendations

**Offensive Pokemon:**
- 252 Atk or SpA / 4 Def or SpD / 252 Spe
- Maximize damage output

**Bulky Attackers:**
- 252 HP / 252 Atk or SpA / 4 Def or SpD
- Survive longer, deal consistent damage

**Support Pokemon:**
- 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD (physical bulk)
- 252 HP / 252 SpD / 4 Def (special bulk)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Not Breaking Shields Fast Enough

**Mistake:** Using weak moves that don't break shields
**Fix:** Always bring super effective moves (2× damage)

### 2. Attacking During Shield Phase

**Mistake:** Wasting turns attacking when shield is up
**Fix:** Use support moves (Screech, Acid Spray) during shield phase

### 3. Not Using Support Moves

**Mistake:** Everyone attacks, no one supports
**Fix:** One player uses Screech/Acid Spray, others attack

### 4. Terastallizing Too Early

**Mistake:** Tera on Turn 1 without thinking
**Fix:** Save Tera for late game (when boss is low HP)

### 5. Bringing Wrong Type

**Mistake:** Using Pokemon weak to raid boss
**Fix:** Always check raid boss Tera Type before starting
