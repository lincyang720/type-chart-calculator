# Mewtwo Weakness in Pokémon GO: 10th Anniversary Timed Research

Pokémon GO's **10th Anniversary Celebration** includes free Timed Research that leads to a **Mewtwo** encounter. The official announcement schedules the research from **August 12, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. PDT** through **September 6, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time**. Complete the tasks and claim their rewards before the local deadline. [Read Pokémon GO's official announcement](https://pokemongo.com/news/10th-anniversary-celebration).

This guide answers the type-chart question behind searches such as “Mewtwo weakness Pokémon GO.” It separates the official event details from Type Matchup's standard type-chart interpretation. It does not claim a specific live moveset, raid counter ranking, damage breakpoint, or outcome.

## Official event facts vs. Type Matchup planning

**Officially confirmed:** The research is free and leads to a Mewtwo with maxed-out stats and a Special Background. Pokémon GO also says that the Mewtwo caught through this Timed Research has the third Mega Level unlocked for Mega Mewtwo Y. The announcement separately says players who caught Mewtwo at the July 9 Times Square event receive Mewtwo with different stats and rewards.

**Type Matchup planning:** [Mewtwo is Psychic-type](/pokemon/mewtwo). In the standard core-series chart, Psychic is weak to Bug, Ghost, and Dark. Pokémon GO's battle system uses its own effectiveness multipliers, move availability, and formats, so type advantage is a useful starting point—not a promise that any named attacker is the best choice in a live battle.

## What to do before the deadline

The official announcement gives a local-time expiry for the research, so avoid waiting until the final day to begin it.

1. Check the Timed Research in Pokémon GO and complete its tasks before September 6 at 11:59 p.m. local time.
2. Claim the encounter before the deadline; unclaimed Timed Research rewards expire.
3. Use Bug, Ghost, or Dark as the standard-chart types to investigate if you are preparing for a Mewtwo matchup.
4. Check the live move and battle format before spending resources. Type advantage alone does not establish a Pokémon GO counter ranking.
