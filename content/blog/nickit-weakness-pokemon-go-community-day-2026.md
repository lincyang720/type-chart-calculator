Pokémon GO says that Nickit will appear more frequently in the wild during the three-hour Community Day. If you are lucky, you may encounter a Shiny Nickit or one with a Special Background. It also lists these event details:

- Evolve Nickit during the event or up to four hours after it ends for a Thievul with the Charged Attack Icy Wind.
- Catching Pokémon awards 3× Stardust and 2× Candy during the event window.
- Incense lasts three hours during the event, excluding Daily Adventure Incense.
- From 2:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. local time, regular Lure Modules have a high chance to attract Nickit, and the additional Special Trade and 50% Stardust trade-cost bonuses apply.

The announcement does not state a guaranteed Shiny encounter, an individual-value outcome, a best raid counter, or a PvP ranking. Treat those as separate questions from the announced schedule and the standard chart.
