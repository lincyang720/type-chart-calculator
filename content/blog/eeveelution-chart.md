# Eeveelution Chart

Here is the quick **Eeveelution chart**: Eevee is Normal, and its eight evolutions cover eight different types. Use the weakness column when choosing a counter, and the immunity or resistance column when choosing a switch-in.

| Pokémon | Type | Standard weaknesses | Key resistance or immunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eevee | Normal | Fighting | Immune to Ghost |
| Vaporeon | Water | Electric, Grass | Resists Fire, Water, Ice, Steel |
| Jolteon | Electric | Ground | Resists Electric, Flying, Steel |
| Flareon | Fire | Water, Ground, Rock | Resists Fire, Grass, Ice, Bug, Steel, Fairy |
| Espeon | Psychic | Bug, Ghost, Dark | Resists Fighting, Psychic |
| Umbreon | Dark | Fighting, Bug, Fairy | Immune to Psychic; resists Ghost and Dark |
| Leafeon | Grass | Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, Bug | Resists Water, Electric, Grass, Ground |
| Glaceon | Ice | Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel | Resists Ice |
| Sylveon | Fairy | Poison, Steel | Immune to Dragon; resists Fighting, Bug, Dark |

The table uses the standard main-series type chart. A move's actual result can change with abilities, held items, weather, Terastallization, or a game-specific battle rule, so verify unusual cases in the [Pokémon type chart](/pokemon/type-chart).

## Eeveelutions by type

| Type | Eeveelution | What makes the type useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Eevee | The baseline choice with a simple weakness and a Ghost immunity. |
| Water | Vaporeon | A bulky Water option that handles Fire, Water, Ice, and Steel attacks well. |
| Electric | Jolteon | The narrowest defensive weakness in the family: Ground is the matchup to cover. |
| Fire | Flareon | Strong resistance coverage, balanced by weaknesses to Water, Ground, and Rock. |
| Psychic | Espeon | Fast-looking Psychic identity with useful Fighting and Psychic resistances. |
| Dark | Umbreon | A defensive Dark profile with a Psychic immunity and a different set of answers from Espeon. |
| Grass | Leafeon | Four helpful resistances, but several common attacking types to watch. |
| Ice | Glaceon | A clear Ice identity that needs protection from Fire, Fighting, Rock, and Steel. |
| Fairy | Sylveon | The family member that blanks Dragon attacks and checks Fighting, Bug, and Dark pressure. |

## How to use the chart in a matchup

### If you are picking an evolution

Start with the type you need, not the Pokémon name. Jolteon is the cleanest choice when the main concern is a Flying or Water matchup and you can protect it from Ground. Sylveon is the direct Fairy option when Dragon immunity matters. Umbreon is the better Dark-flavored defensive choice when you want a Psychic immunity rather than Espeon's Psychic damage profile.

### If you are countering an Eeveelution

Read the exact row. Do not use “Eevee weakness” as a substitute for the evolved form's weakness. For example, Fighting is super effective against Eevee but does not have the same meaning against Espeon, Umbreon, or Sylveon. The evolution's type is the first filter.

### If the Pokémon has an extra battle mechanic

Use this as the type baseline, then check the exact game and format. Terastallization, abilities, and special forms can change what you should bring. The [type calculator](/calculator) is the quickest way to compare one or two defending types against every attacking type.

## Eeveelution chart takeaway

The eight evolutions are a compact tour of the type system: Water, Electric, Fire, Psychic, Dark, Grass, Ice, and Fairy. Pick by the matchup you need, then cover the weakness shown in the table. That keeps the chart useful for both collecting and team planning without treating every Eeveelution as if it shared Eevee's Normal typing.
