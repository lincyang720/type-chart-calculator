# Every 4× Weakness in Gen 9 (And How to Play Around Them)

A 4× weakness is 2× times 2×. Both defending types are weak to the same attack, and the Pokémon that stays in takes it. This page lists all 53 dual-type pairs on the Generation 6–9 chart, Fairy included. Eight are 4× to two attacks. Four have no Pokémon through Generation 9. A single type stops at 2×: pure Rock takes Fighting at 2×, and Rock/Dark takes it at 4×.

Multiply with the [dual-type chart guide](/blog/how-to-read-dual-type-chart), then place the pair on a sheet with the [team-building guide](/blog/pokemon-type-calculator-team-building). Confirm it in the [Dual Type Calculator](/calculator), or filter the [dual-type chart](/dual-type-chart) with **Has a 4× weakness**. Older names such as Heatran use this same chart. Pokémon GO uses 1.6× and 2.56×. Raids belong on the [Pokémon GO type chart](/blog/pokemon-go-type-chart-2026).

## Read this before the buckets

A normal Tera type replaces both defending types. Fire Tera [Charizard](/pokemon/charizard) still takes Rock at 2×. Steel or Ground Tera takes Rock at 0.5×. Stellar Tera, in Tera Raid Battles, keeps the original defense. Read the new type on the [calculator](/calculator).

Stealth Rock deals half of maximum HP to every pair in the Rock section. Heavy-Duty Boots or removal comes before the Rock move. A resistance Berry halves one super-effective hit, so the 4× lands at 2× once. Yache is Ice, Charti is Rock, and Shuca is Ground.

Levitate, Flash Fire, and Sap Sipper can make the product 0×. The [ability chart](/pokemon/type-chart-with-abilities) prints the ability beside the multiplier. Mold Breaker Earthquake from [Excadrill](/pokemon/excadrill) hits Levitate and misses a Flying type. Thousand Arrows hits both. Rain turns a 4× Fire hit into 2× before stats. Sun multiplies Fire by 1.5×, so that hit lands at 6×. The [STAB guide](/blog/stab-calculator-guide) adds the separate 1.5× when the attacker shares the move's type.

## Use the list on a team sheet

Load the six into the [team calculator](/pokemon/team-calculator). The red box lists attacks that hit somebody and hit nobody at 0.5×, 0.25×, or 0×. A 4× can sit outside that box when a teammate resists it. In the [team-building guide](/blog/pokemon-type-calculator-team-building), Kingambit resists Garchomp's Ice, the box stays quiet, and Ice Shard still removes Garchomp.

Check which member is 4×, which member resists it, and whether that resist outspeeds the priority version: Ice Shard, Grassy Glide, Mach Punch, Bullet Punch, Aqua Jet, or First Impression. The [type coverage calculator](/type-coverage-calculator) is the attacking side of the same sheet.

## FAQ

### How many dual types take 4× damage in Gen 9?

53 pairs on the Generation 6–9 chart. Eight take 4× from two attacks. A single type stops at 2×. Four pairs have no Pokémon through Generation 9: Fire/Ice, Normal/Ice, Normal/Rock, and Normal/Steel.

### What does a 4× weakness mean?

The attack is super effective against both defending types, so 2× times 2× is 4×. A weakness times a resistance is 1×. An immunity makes the product 0×.

### How do you play around a 4× weakness?

Switch to a resist or an immunity, Terastallize into a type that resists that attack, or block repeated entries with Heavy-Duty Boots, removal, or a resistance Berry. The Berry halves one hit, so it lands at 2×.

### Which typings are 4× weak to two attacks?

Fire/Rock (Water and Ground), Ground/Rock (Water and Grass), Rock/Steel (Fighting and Ground), Ice/Rock (Fighting and Steel), Ice/Steel (Fire and Fighting), Ice/Bug (Fire and Rock), Grass/Bug (Fire and Flying), and Psychic/Ghost (Ghost and Dark).

### Does Terastallization remove a 4× weakness?

A normal Tera type replaces both defending types. Fire Tera Charizard takes Rock at 2×. Steel or Ground Tera takes Rock at 0.5×. Stellar Tera in raids keeps the original defense.

### Are these 4× weaknesses the same in Pokémon GO?

No. This chart uses 2× and 4×. Pokémon GO uses 1.6× and 2.56×, and an immunity deals reduced damage. Use the [Pokémon GO type chart](/blog/pokemon-go-type-chart-2026).
