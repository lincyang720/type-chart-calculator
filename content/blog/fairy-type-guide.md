# Fairy Type Guide - Why Fairy is the Best Defensive Type

Fairy type was introduced in Generation 6 to nerf Dragons. It succeeded — and became the most dominant defensive type in competitive Pokemon. Here's why.

## Fairy Type Resistances & Immunities

Fairy resists **3 types** and is **immune to 1**:

**Resists:**
- Fighting (0.5×)
- Bug (0.5×)
- Dark (0.5×)

**Immune to:**
- Dragon (0×) — Complete immunity

This is huge. Fairy walls three of the most common offensive types (Fighting, Dark, Dragon) while taking zero damage from Dragon moves.

## Fairy Type Weaknesses

Fairy is weak to only **2 types**:
- Poison (2×)
- Steel (2×)

Both are relatively uncommon offensive types. Most teams don't run dedicated Poison or Steel attackers, making Fairy extremely safe to switch in.

## Why Fairy is the Best Defensive Type

### 1. Dragon Immunity
Dragon was the most broken offensive type in Gens 1-5. Fairy completely shuts it down. A Garchomp locked into Outrage does **0 damage** to any Fairy type.

### 2. Resists Common Offensive Types
Fighting and Dark are everywhere in competitive play:
- **Fighting:** Close Combat, Superpower, Drain Punch
- **Dark:** Knock Off, Sucker Punch, Crunch

Fairy resists all of them.

### 3. Uncommon Weaknesses
Poison and Steel coverage is rare. Most physical attackers run Fighting/Dark/Ground coverage. Most special attackers run Fire/Ice/Electric. Neither hits Fairy super effectively.

### 4. Excellent Dual-Type Combinations
Fairy pairs incredibly well with other defensive types:

**Fairy/Steel (Magearna, Zacian):**
- 11 resistances, 2 immunities
- Only weak to Fire and Ground
- Best defensive typing in the game

**Fairy/Water (Azumarill, Primarina):**
- Resists Fire, Water, Ice, Fighting, Bug, Dark, Dragon
- Only weak to Grass, Electric, Poison

**Fairy/Psychic (Gardevoir, Hatterene):**
- Immune to Dragon
- Resists Fighting and Psychic
- Weak to Ghost, Poison, Steel

## Best Fairy Type Pokemon

### Clefable
**Type:** Fairy  
**Ability:** Magic Guard / Unaware  
**Role:** Special wall, cleric, setup sweeper

Clefable is the most versatile Fairy type. Magic Guard prevents chip damage from Stealth Rock, Toxic, and weather. Unaware ignores stat boosts, making it the ultimate Dragon Dance counter.

**Standard Set:**
- Moonblast
- Soft-Boiled
- Stealth Rock / Thunder Wave
- Calm Mind / Knock Off

### Magearna
**Type:** Steel/Fairy  
**Ability:** Soul-Heart  
**Role:** Special attacker, tank

Steel/Fairy is the best defensive typing in Pokemon. Magearna resists 11 types and is immune to Dragon and Poison. Soul-Heart boosts Special Attack by 1 stage whenever any Pokemon faints.

**Standard Set:**
- Fleur Cannon
- Flash Cannon
- Volt Switch
- Focus Blast

### Azumarill
**Type:** Water/Fairy  
**Ability:** Huge Power  
**Role:** Physical attacker, belly drum sweeper

Huge Power doubles Azumarill's Attack stat, making it hit like a truck despite mediocre base stats. Aqua Jet priority bypasses faster threats.

**Belly Drum Set:**
- Belly Drum (+6 Attack)
- Aqua Jet (priority)
- Play Rough
- Superpower

### Sylveon
**Type:** Fairy  
**Ability:** Pixilate  
**Role:** Special attacker

Pixilate turns Normal moves into Fairy moves with a 1.2× boost. Hyper Voice becomes a 117 BP Fairy move that hits through Substitute.

**Standard Set:**
- Hyper Voice (Pixilate)
- Mystical Fire
- Psyshock
- Calm Mind

[Check Sylveon's full type matchups →](/pokemon/sylveon)

### Grimmsnarl
**Type:** Dark/Fairy  
**Ability:** Prankster  
**Role:** Support, screens setter

Prankster gives +1 priority to status moves. Grimmsnarl sets up Reflect and Light Screen before opponents can move.

**Screens Set:**
- Reflect
- Light Screen
- Taunt
- Spirit Break / Foul Play

## Fairy Type Moves

### Best Fairy Attacking Moves
- **Moonblast** (95 BP, 100% accuracy, 30% chance to lower SpA)
- **Play Rough** (90 BP, 90% accuracy, 10% chance to lower Attack)
- **Dazzling Gleam** (80 BP, 100% accuracy, hits both opponents in doubles)
- **Fleur Cannon** (130 BP, 90% accuracy, lowers user's SpA by 2 stages)

### Best Fairy Status Moves
- **Misty Terrain** (prevents status conditions for 5 turns, halves Dragon damage)
- **Aromatherapy** (heals all status conditions on your team)
- **Moonlight** (restores 50% HP, 66% in sun, 25% in rain/sand/snow)

## Countering Fairy Types

### Poison Types
- **Toxapex** (Poison/Water) — Walls most Fairy types, Toxic stall
- **Amoonguss** (Grass/Poison) — Spore + Sludge Bomb
- **Galarian Weezing** (Poison/Fairy) — Neutralizing Gas shuts down abilities

### Steel Types
- **Heatran** (Fire/Steel) — Resists Fairy, hits back with Magma Storm
- **Ferrothorn** (Grass/Steel) — Walls physical Fairies, sets hazards
- **Corviknight** (Flying/Steel) — Defogs hazards, walls Fairy attacks

### Coverage Moves
- **Sludge Bomb / Gunk Shot** (Poison)
- **Iron Head / Flash Cannon** (Steel)
- **Fire Blast / Flamethrower** (hits Steel/Fairy types)

## Fairy Type Strategy Tips

### 1. Use Fairy Types as Pivot Points
Fairy's resistances make it easy to switch in. Use Fairy types to pivot into Fighting, Dark, or Dragon attacks.

### 2. Pair with Steel Types
Steel resists Fairy's weaknesses (Poison, Steel). Fairy resists Steel's weaknesses (Fighting, Fire, Ground). They cover each other perfectly.

### 3. Watch for Poison Coverage
Many Pokemon run Poison coverage specifically to hit Fairy types:
- Garchomp with Poison Jab
- Landorus with Sludge Wave
- Tapu Koko with Poison Jab

### 4. Abuse Dragon Immunity
If your opponent has a Dragon-type locked into Outrage or Dragon Claw, switch in your Fairy type for a free turn.

## Conclusion

Fairy is the best defensive type in Pokemon because:
1. **Dragon immunity** — shuts down the former king of types
2. **Resists Fighting, Dark, Bug** — common offensive types
3. **Weak to Poison, Steel** — uncommon offensive types
4. **Excellent dual-type combos** — Steel/Fairy, Water/Fairy, Psychic/Fairy

If you're building a defensive core, include a Fairy type. It's the most reliable defensive typing in competitive Pokemon.
