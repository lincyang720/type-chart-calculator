# Steel Type Pokemon - The Ultimate Defensive Typing

Steel type has the most resistances of any type in Pokemon: **11 resistances and 1 immunity**. This makes it the ultimate defensive typing for competitive play.

## Steel Type Resistances & Immunities

Steel resists **11 types** and is **immune to 1**:

**Resists (0.5×):**
- Normal
- Flying
- Rock
- Bug
- Steel
- Grass
- Psychic
- Ice
- Dragon
- Fairy

**Immune to:**
- Poison (0×)

This is absurd. Steel resists more than half of all types in the game. It's the backbone of every defensive team.

## Steel Type Weaknesses

Steel is weak to only **3 types**:
- Fire (2×)
- Fighting (2×)
- Ground (2×)

All three are common offensive types, which is Steel's main drawback. But with proper team support, Steel types can wall almost anything.

## Why Steel is the Best Defensive Type

### 1. Resists 11 Types
Steel resists more types than any other. This includes:
- **Ice** — Most types are weak to Ice, but Steel resists it
- **Dragon** — Steel walls Dragon attacks
- **Fairy** — Steel is one of only two types that resists Fairy
- **Psychic** — Steel resists Psychic, making it a great check

### 2. Immune to Poison
Poison immunity means:
- No Toxic stall
- No Poison damage
- Can switch into Toxic Spikes without penalty

### 3. Excellent Dual-Type Combinations
Steel pairs incredibly well with other types to cover its weaknesses:

**Steel/Fairy (Magearna, Zacian):**
- 11 resistances, 2 immunities (Dragon, Poison)
- Only weak to Fire and Ground
- **Best defensive typing in the game**

**Steel/Flying (Skarmory, Corviknight):**
- Immune to Ground (Steel's biggest weakness)
- Resists 10 types
- Only weak to Fire and Electric

**Steel/Psychic (Metagross, Jirachi):**
- Resists 9 types
- Weak to Fire, Ground, Ghost, Dark

**Steel/Dragon (Dialga, Duraludon):**
- Resists 10 types
- Only weak to Fighting and Ground

## Best Steel Type Pokemon

### Corviknight
**Type:** Flying/Steel  
**Ability:** Pressure / Mirror Armor  
**Role:** Physical wall, defogger

Corviknight is the best defensive Steel type in OU. Flying/Steel gives it a Ground immunity, and Mirror Armor reflects stat drops (Intimidate, Defog, etc.).

**Standard Set:**
- Brave Bird
- Roost
- Defog
- U-turn / Iron Head

### Ferrothorn
**Type:** Grass/Steel  
**Ability:** Iron Barbs  
**Role:** Physical wall, hazard setter

Ferrothorn has the highest Defense of any Steel type (131 base). Iron Barbs damages attackers on contact. Grass/Steel resists Water, Electric, and Grass — perfect for walling Water types.

**Standard Set:**
- Stealth Rock
- Leech Seed
- Power Whip
- Gyro Ball / Knock Off

[Check Ferrothorn's full type matchups →](/pokemon/ferrothorn)

### Heatran
**Type:** Fire/Steel  
**Ability:** Flash Fire  
**Role:** Special attacker, trapper

Heatran is the only Fire/Steel type. This gives it a **4× resistance to Ice** and immunity to Fire (via Flash Fire). It's the best Fairy-type check in the game.

**Standard Set:**
- Magma Storm (traps opponents)
- Earth Power
- Flash Cannon
- Taunt / Stealth Rock

[Check Heatran's full type matchups →](/pokemon/heatran)

### Metagross
**Type:** Steel/Psychic  
**Ability:** Clear Body  
**Role:** Physical attacker, tank

Metagross has 135 base Attack and 130 base Defense. Clear Body prevents stat drops (Intimidate, Sticky Web, etc.). Excellent bulk and offensive presence.

**Standard Set:**
- Meteor Mash (STAB, 90 BP, 20% chance to raise Attack)
- Earthquake
- Zen Headbutt
- Ice Punch / Bullet Punch

### Magearna
**Type:** Steel/Fairy  
**Ability:** Soul-Heart  
**Role:** Special attacker, tank

Steel/Fairy is the best defensive typing in Pokemon. Magearna resists 11 types and is immune to Dragon and Poison. Soul-Heart boosts Special Attack by 1 stage whenever any Pokemon faints.

**Standard Set:**
- Fleur Cannon
- Flash Cannon
- Volt Switch
- Focus Blast

### Scizor
**Type:** Bug/Steel  
**Ability:** Technician  
**Role:** Physical attacker, priority user

Technician boosts moves with 60 BP or less by 1.5×. Bullet Punch becomes 60 BP with STAB and Technician (90 effective BP priority move).

**Standard Set:**
- Bullet Punch (priority)
- U-turn
- Swords Dance
- Knock Off / Superpower

**Role:** Mixed attacker, tank

Aegislash has 150/150 defenses in Shield Forme and 150/150 offenses in Blade Forme. Steel/Ghost gives it 3 immunities (Normal, Fighting, Poison).

## Steel Type Moves

### Best Steel Attacking Moves
- **Meteor Mash** (90 BP, 90% accuracy, 20% chance to raise Attack)
- **Iron Head** (80 BP, 100% accuracy, 30% chance to flinch)
- **Flash Cannon** (80 BP, 100% accuracy, 10% chance to lower SpD)
- **Bullet Punch** (40 BP, priority +1, boosted by Technician)
- **Gyro Ball** (variable BP, stronger when user is slower)

### Best Steel Status Moves
- **Stealth Rock** (most important move in competitive Pokemon)
- **Iron Defense** (+2 Defense)
- **Metal Burst** (reflects damage taken)

## Countering Steel Types

### Fire Types
Fire is the most reliable Steel counter:
- **Heatran** (Fire/Steel) — Resists Steel, immune to Fire
- **Volcarona** (Bug/Fire) — Quiver Dance sweeper, Fire Blast OHKOs most Steel types
- **Cinderace** (Fire) — Pyro Ball hits like a truck

### Fighting Types
Fighting is super effective against Steel:
- **Close Combat** (120 BP, lowers Defense and SpD)
- **Superpower** (120 BP, lowers Attack and Defense)
- **Urshifu** (Fighting/Dark) — Wicked Blow ignores stat changes

### Ground Types
Ground is the most common Steel counter:
- **Earthquake** (100 BP, 100% accuracy, most common coverage move)
- **Landorus-T** (Ground/Flying) — Intimidate + Earthquake
- **Garchomp** (Dragon/Ground) — Earthquake + Swords Dance

### Coverage Moves
Most Pokemon run Fire, Fighting, or Ground coverage specifically to hit Steel types:
- **Fire Blast / Flamethrower**
- **Close Combat / Superpower**
- **Earthquake / Earth Power**

## Steel Type in Competitive Tiers

### OU (OverUsed)
Steel types are essential in OU:
- Corviknight (S-tier defogger)
- Ferrothorn (S-tier physical wall)
- Heatran (A-tier special wall)
- Magearna (banned to Ubers in some gens)

### Ubers
Even in Ubers, Steel types are everywhere:
- Zacian-Crowned (Fairy/Steel, most broken Pokemon in Gen 8)
- Necrozma-Dusk Mane (Psychic/Steel)
- Dialga (Steel/Dragon)

### VGC (Doubles)
Steel types are common in VGC:
- Metagross (Meteor Mash + Protect)
- Ferrothorn (Trick Room setter)
- Zacian (restricted legendary)

## Steel Type Strategy Tips

### 1. Use Steel Types as Defensive Pivots
Steel's 11 resistances make it easy to switch in. Use Steel types to pivot into Ice, Dragon, Fairy, or Psychic attacks.

### 2. Pair with Ground Immunities
Steel is weak to Ground. Pair it with Flying types, Levitate users, or Air Balloon holders:
- Corviknight (Flying/Steel)
- Skarmory (Flying/Steel)
- Bronzong (Steel/Psychic with Levitate)

### 3. Watch for Fire Coverage
Most teams run Fire coverage specifically to hit Steel types. Be careful switching into:
- Fire Blast / Flamethrower
- Magma Storm (Heatran)
- Overheat

### 4. Set Stealth Rock
Many Steel types learn Stealth Rock. Use them to set hazards early:
- Ferrothorn
- Heatran
- Metagross
- Excadrill

- Dragon, Poison

This typing walls almost everything. The only common weaknesses are Fire and Ground, which can be covered by teammates.
